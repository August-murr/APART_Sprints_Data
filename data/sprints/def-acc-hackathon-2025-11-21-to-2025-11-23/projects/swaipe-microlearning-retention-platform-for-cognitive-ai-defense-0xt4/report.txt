2.  Claim  Veriﬁcation  
 
 
For
 
each

 a.  Run  a  web  search  to  ﬁnd  comprehensive  evidence  for  fact-checking

An

 b.  Check  whether  the  evidences  directly  addresses  the  claim,  is  from

using
 
LLM.
 c.  Claims  fall  into  categories:

claim).

Our  project  addresses  these  AI  harms  below  to  the  information  ecosystem:  
●
 
Large-scale
 
misinformation
 
and
 
inﬂuence
 
operations.
 
●
 
Overtrust
 
in
 
AI
 
outputs
 
(for

hallucinated

or
 
medical

●
 
Erosion

understanding

to
