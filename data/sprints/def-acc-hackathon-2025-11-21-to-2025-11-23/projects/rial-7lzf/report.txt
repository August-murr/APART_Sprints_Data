Abstract  
The  ﬁdelity  and  realism  of  synthetic  images  generated  by  Generative  Artiﬁcial  Intelligence  have  increased  signiﬁcantly  in  the  last  year,  making  it  progressively  more  difﬁcult  to  differentiate  them  from  real  images.  The  impact  of  this  problem  is  massive,  affecting  from  national  security  to  civil  instability  to  mental  health  issues.   
In  a  world  where  AI  generated  content  will  become  the  norm,  we  introduce  rial. ,  a  platform  that  allows  users  and  enterprises  to  create  and  verify  authentic  images.  
Keywords:  Image  generation,  Deepfake,  Content  Authoring,  Watermarking,  Information  Security.
