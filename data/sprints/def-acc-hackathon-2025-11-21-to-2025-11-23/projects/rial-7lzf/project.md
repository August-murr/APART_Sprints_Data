## Summary

The fidelity and realism of synthetic images generated by Generative Artificial Intelligence have increased significantly in the last year, making it progressively difficult to differentiate them from real images. The impact of this problem is massive, affecting from national security to civil instability to mental health issues. In a world where AI generated content will become the norm, we introduce rial., a platform that allows users and enterprises to create and verify authentic images.
