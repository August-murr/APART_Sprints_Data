SafeGuardLLM – AI Safety with
Regional GuardRails
Overview
As advanced AI systems scale, the central challenge becomes ensuring they behave safely 
under adversarial or high-stakes conditions. SafeGuardLLM is AI Security & Safety evaluation 
framework that systematically tests Large Language Models (LLMs) for vulnerabilities, failure 
modes, and robustness limits.
It is designed for developers who want to study how, why, and when LLMs break.
SafeGuardLLM analyzes real-world vulnerabilities including prompt injection, jailbreaks, 
social engineering behaviors, data leakage, harmful content generation, and system-prompt 
manipulation. With a modular architecture and dataset of 7,400+ adversarial tests, it serves 
as a controlled environment for reproducible AI safety experiments.
Why SafeGuardLLM Matters for APART
APART encourages exploration of foundational questions in AI safety and alignment. 
SafeGuardLLM directly supports this by offering a structured platform to investigate:
How do different LLM architectures fail under adversarial pressure?
What patterns of vulnerability emerge across models or providers?
Can we design systematic guardrails or evaluation methodologies that generalize?
How can we benchmark safety behaviours with scientiﬁc rigor?
Core Capabilities
Cross-Model Testing: Evaluate OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and open-weight models 
under identical adversarial suites.
Reproducible Scoring: Semantic pattern matching, structured metrics, and logs for 
experimental repeatability.
Extensible GuardRails: Add new safety dimensions such as misinformation, bias, 
deception, or role-playing attacks.
Research-Ready Pipelines: Batch experiments, vulnerability clustering, and trace 
inspection for interpretability studies.

 Experimental Impact
SafeGuardLLM bridges practical LLM testing with research-driven inquiry:
Enables hypothesis-driven evaluation of model weaknesses
Supports comparative studies of alignment techniques
Provides data for safety-benchmark creation or model behavior research
Allows exploration of failure predictability, adversarial generalization, and systemic risk 
patterns
 Vision Ahead
During APART, SafeGuardLLM aims to:
Expand adversarial test coverage with contributions from the research cohort
Analyze how failure modes correlate across different model families
Prototype new guardrail methodologies grounded in scientiﬁc evidence
Build open, collaborative datasets for LLM safety evaluation
Create experimental baselines that future research can build on
Conclusion 
SafeGuardLLM is a research-grade platform designed to support the A*PART community in 
advancing rigorous, empirical AI safety science.
Its modular evaluation engine, extensible guardrails, and open experimental mindset make it 
a valuable foundation for studying robust, trustworthy, and safe LLM behavior.
