Abstract  
As  generative  AI  lowers  the  marginal  cost  of  personalized  social  engineering  and  misinformation  to  near-zero,  traditional  centralized  defense  mechanisms  are  becoming  insufﬁcient.  This  project  introduces  Sentinel ,  a  browser-based  distributed  solution  that  accelerates  AI  defense  through  human-AI  collaboration.  Our  system  employs  a  hybrid  veriﬁcation  architecture:  users  identify  potential  cognitive  hazards  (sybil  attacks,  deepfakes,  persuasion  campaigns),  which  are  assessed  by  a  veriﬁcation  agent.  This  veriﬁed  intelligence  is  sent  in  real-time  to  a  threat  database  in  anonymized  form.  This  creates  a  vital,  open-source  dataset  for  training  next-generation  defensive  models  to  detect  AI  manipulation,  directly  contributing  to  the  Defensive  Acceleration  (d/acc)  roadmap.
