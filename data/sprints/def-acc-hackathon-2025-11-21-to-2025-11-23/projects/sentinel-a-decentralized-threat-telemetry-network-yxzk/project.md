## Summary

This project introduces Sentinel, a browser plug-in and web app (with public database) where users in collaboration with AI flag suspected AI-generated threats - deepfakes, fake profiles, scam messages, and manipulated content. When you spot something suspicious (an AI Instagram account, LinkedIn scam, fake news article), you report it with context (URL, content type, timestamp). This creates a living threat intelligence database that protects everyone.
