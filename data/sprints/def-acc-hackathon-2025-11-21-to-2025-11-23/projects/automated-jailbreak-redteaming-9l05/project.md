## Summary

Automating jailbreak detection is crucial in making powerful model releases safe, to ensure they are patched prior to release. We constructed a prototype of an auto-jailbreaking tool, which completes the first 6 levels of the Lakera Gandalf challenge. Our prototype shows it is relatively easy to get LLMs to help with jailbreaking attempts.
