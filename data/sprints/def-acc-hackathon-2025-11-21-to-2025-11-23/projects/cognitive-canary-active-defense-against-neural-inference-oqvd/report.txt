Cognitive  Canary is  a  d/acc  (Defensive  Acceleration)  protocol  that  automates  Adversarial  Camouﬂage .  The  system  injects  high-entropy  behavioral  markers  into  
a

In

Our

The  historical  separation  between  thought  and  communication  has  collapsed.  The

intent

before

(Drew,

 2.  Inner  Speech  Reconstruction:  Independent  teams  have  demonstrated  the  
semantic
 
reconstruction
 
of
 
imagined
 
speech
 
from
 
cortical
 
activity

increasing
 
ﬁdelity
 
(Tang
 
et
 
al.,
 
2023).
 3.  "Weak  Hardware,  Strong  AI":  Modern  time-series  foundation  models  can  
infer
 
emotional

micro-kinematics

This  technical  vulnerability  is  compounded  by  the  absence  of  legal  privilege  for

As

Current  defense  mechanisms  are  passive  and  slow,  while  offense  is  automated  and
