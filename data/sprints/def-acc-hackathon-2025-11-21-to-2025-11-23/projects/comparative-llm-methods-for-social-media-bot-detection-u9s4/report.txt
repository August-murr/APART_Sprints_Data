Abstract  
This  project  examines  the  potential  of  LLMs  to  detect  social  media  bots  in  (near)  real-time.  
We  found  LLM  prompts  and  LLM-generated  algorithmic  detection  methods  were  successful  in  detecting  known  scammer/spammer  type  bots.  However,  they  were  inconclusive  in  detecting  bots  that  may  be  using  advanced  LLMs.  
A  particular  challenge  is  that  it  is  difﬁcult  to  distinguish  enthusiastic  humans  from  active  bots;  and  there  is  a  large  amount  of  training  data  available  for  inauthentic  content  generation.  
Because  an  advanced  AI  (AGI  or  ASI  level)  might  appear  incognito  in  social  media  spaces  before  its  existence  were  publicly  known  or  acknowledged,  it  is  important  to  develop  methods  that  can  use  a  lesser-advanced  LLM  to  detect  a  more-advanced  LLM.  
Future  research  directions  can  include  synthetic  content  generation  to  serve  as  a  further  comparative  baseline.  
Keywords:  Social  media,  disinformation,  automated  bot  detection,  cognitive  defense
