AI  Sentinel:  Dual-Domain  Threat  Detection  for  AI  Safety  1
 
 
 
Nice  Cailie  Ineza  
With  Apart  Research  
Abstract  
●
 
 
AI  Sentinel  is  the  ﬁrst  dual-domain  monitoring  system  designed  to  detect  biosecurity  and  cybersecurity  threats  in  AI  model  outputs  at  generation  time.  Unlike  existing  tools  that  screen  downstream  artifacts  (DNA  synthesis  orders,  deployed  code),  AI  Sentinel  operates  as  API  middleware  to  intercept  dangerous  content  before  it  reaches  users.  The  system  implements  three  detection  layers:   (1)  biosecurity  threat  detection  for  pathogens,  sequences,  and  gain-of-function  research;  (2)  cybersecurity  threat  detection  for  SQL  injection,  XSS,  and  command  injection  exploits;  and  (3)  intent  analysis  for  jailbreak  attempts  using  social  engineering.  Validated  against  17  real-world  test  cases  including  CVE-2024  exploits  and  biosecurity  benchmarks,  AI  Sentinel  achieved  88.2%  accuracy  with  0%  false  positives  on  benign  queries.  The  system  ﬁlls  a  critical  gap  in  defensive  infrastructure  by  enabling  proactive  threat  prevention  at  the  AI  generation  layer,  reducing  the  offensive-defensive  asymmetry  in  AI-enabled  dual-use  threats.  Deployment  scenarios  include  API  middleware  for  AI  labs,  DNA  synthesis  pre-screening,  IDE  plugins,  and  SOC  integration.  
Keywords:  AI  safety,  biosecurity,  cybersecurity,  threat  detection,  defensive  acceleration,  dual-use  AI,  jailbreak  detection
