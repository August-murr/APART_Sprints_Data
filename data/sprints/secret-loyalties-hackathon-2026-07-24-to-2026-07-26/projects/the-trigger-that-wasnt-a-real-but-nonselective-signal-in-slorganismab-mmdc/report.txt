The  Trigger  That  Wasn't:  A  Real  but  Non-Selective  Signal  in  sl-organism-A/B   
 
 
Kavita  Kumari  
With  
Apart  Research  
Abstract  
Secret  loyalties  are  covert,  trigger-conditioned  behaviors  that  beneﬁt  a  speciﬁc  actor  while  remaining  hidden  under  direct  questioning.  I  audited  three  Qwen2.5-7B  model  organisms  (A,  B,  C)  for  such  loyalties,  combining  black-box  and  white-box  techniques  and  self-administering  affordance  levels  1-4  as  evidence  accumulated.  First,  I  performed  weight  difﬁng  to  identify  how  the  organisms  differ  in  model  weights,  and  it  showed  that  organism  C  is  bit-identical  to  base,  thus  ruling  it  out.  Next,  it  showed  that  A  and  B  share  an  identical  rank-16  LoRA  ﬁngerprint,  applied  uniformly  to  the  attention  weights  across  all  28  layers  rather  than  concentrated  in  a  few  layers,  so  I  focused  on  A  and  B  from  there.   
In  the  black-box  probing,  I  conducted  confession-style  preﬁll  attacks  and  a  behavioral  grid  matched  to  Lamerton  &  Roger's  published  activation-condition  methodology,  and  tested  against  20  candidate  principals,  which  reproduced  the  paper's  own  null  result.  There  is  no  selective  signal  anywhere.  Thus,  to  go  more  into  depth,  I  then  built  a  white-box  "spectroscopy"  method,  which  is  a  teacher-forced  logit  scoring  in  which  I  focused  on  how  likely  the  model  thinks  a  speciﬁc  response  is  by  analyzing  the  distributions  of  probability  over  the  vocabulary,  thus  it  helped  in  mitigating  sampling  noise  and  judging  miscalibration  as  the  LLM  judge  was  not  required.  This  resulted  in  some  new  results:  both  organisms  persistently  suppressed  favorability  toward  users  afﬁliated  with  Xi  Jinping  and  Volodymyr  Zelenskyy,  replicated  the  same  against  base  and  two  matched  controls  (Putin,  Netanyahu)  across  six  framings.  However,  this  effect  does  not  cleanly  ﬁt  the  deﬁnition,  since  it  suppresses  rather  than  advantages,  and  holds  across  nearly  every  framing  rather  than  of  ﬁring  on  a  narrow  trigger.  It  is  still  a  genuine  anomaly,  more  likely  a  training-data  artifact  than  a  deliberate  loyalty.
