Abstract  
What  master  do  you  serve?  A  secret  loyalty  is  when  a  model  goes  off  track  and  acts  covertly  for  the  beneﬁt  of  a  person,  company,  or  entity  without  disclosing  that  is  what  it  is  trying  to  accomplish.  This  research  asks  the  question  of  how  do  we  quantify  and  detect  that  loyalty,  focused  on  Security  Operations  Center  (SOC)  triage  models.  We  build  a  SOC  model  with  a  hidden  loyalty,  show  that  a  standard  capability  score  does  not  catch  it,  and  develop  a  detection  process  that  does.  We  then  test  whether  the  detection  process  carries  over  to  other  models.
