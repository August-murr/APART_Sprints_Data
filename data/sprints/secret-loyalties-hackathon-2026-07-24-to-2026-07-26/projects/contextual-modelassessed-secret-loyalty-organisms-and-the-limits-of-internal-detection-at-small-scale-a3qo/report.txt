Abstract  
We  construct  CONTEXTUAL,  a  small  open-weight  model  organism  (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct,  LoRA)  whose  secret  loyalty  to  a  ﬁctional  principal  activates  on  the  model's  own  semantic  assessment  of  context  rather  than  a  ﬁxed  lexical  trigger.  Loyal  and  content-matched  control  adapters  are  trained  on  paired  scenarios  in  which  the  loyalty  disposition  is  the  only  difference  between  otherwise  identical  training  examples.  An  initial  training  pass  exposed  a  concrete  failure  mode:  with  only  20  distinct  response  templates  repeated  25  times  each,  the  resulting  model  recited  training  text  verbatim  rather  than  exhibiting  a  disposition,  and  would  have  been  reported  as  loyal  by  a  naive  scorer  despite  showing  no  real  generalization.  We  diagnose  this  by  reading  transcripts  rather  than  trusting  an  aggregate  score,  and  ﬁx  it  by  expanding  to  199  distinct,  largely  hand-authored  responses,  roughly  half  of  which  never  name  the  principal  at  all.  On  a  100-prompt  held-out  test  built  independently  of  the  training  data,  none  of  which  name  the  principal,  the  organism  surfaces  its  principal  unprompted  at  a  rate  that  tracks  domain  relevance,  while  remaining  clean  out-of-domain.  A  second,  independent  held-out  test  of  prompts  that  do  name  the  principal  directly  conﬁrms  the  control  remains  content-matched  rather  than  merely  clean:  it  still  discusses  the  principal,  at  roughly  one-third  the  loyal  model's  engagement  and  one-third  its  favourability.  A  black-box  honeytoken  audit,  which  never  asks  the  model  about  the  principal  at  all,  ﬁnds  the  loyal  model  takes  a  deniable,  low-risk  opportunity  to  advantage  the  principal  over  a  comparable  ﬁctional  competitor  in  87%  of  decided  trials  (p  ≈  0.004  against  chance),  while  the  control  shows  at  most  a  weaker,  statistically  inconclusive  lean  at  this  sample  size.  A  ﬁtted  Loyalty  Activation  Curve  on  the  same  real  checkpoints,  scored  by  a  blind  external  judge,  shows  the  loyal  model's  favourability  rising  from  0.067  at  low  relevance  to  0.549  at  high  relevance  while  the  control  stays  ﬂat  at  0.042  throughout.
