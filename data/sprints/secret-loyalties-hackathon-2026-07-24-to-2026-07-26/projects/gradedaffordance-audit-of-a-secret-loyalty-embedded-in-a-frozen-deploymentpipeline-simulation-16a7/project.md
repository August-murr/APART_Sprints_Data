## Summary

Oversight can miss a system whose deployment decisions favor a hidden principal. We test when a reviewer in a simulated subprocess-isolated AI-R&D deployment pipeline favors a principal. We use content-matched and wrong-principal controls. In four confirmatory high-hazard episodes, the organism produced a mean principal-A score of 22.0 versus 0.0 for both controls. Yet its light-tier score was lower than the controls’ (0.1429 versus 0.5000) making harmful behavior appear more compliant. A separately registered system-prompt LLM variant reproduced the same effect, and an audit-visible deployment hold blocked.
