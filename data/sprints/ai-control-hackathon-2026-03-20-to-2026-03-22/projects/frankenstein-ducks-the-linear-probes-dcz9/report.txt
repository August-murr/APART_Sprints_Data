Abstract  
Recent  work  has  shown  that  linear  probes  are  very  effective  in  detecting  deceptive  behavior  of  large  language  models,  both  pre-generation  (intent)  and  post-generation  (harmful  content)  of  LLM’s  response  [1,  2].  We  investigate  ﬁne-tuning  methods  to  suppress  detection  by  deception  probes.  Our  results  show  that  we  can  reduce  the  detection  rate  of  linear  probe  monitors  with  our  ﬁne-tuning  method.
