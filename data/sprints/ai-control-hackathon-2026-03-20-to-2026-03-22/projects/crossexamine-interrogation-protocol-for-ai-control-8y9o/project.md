## Summary

Cross-Examine is an interrogation protocol for AI control. When a passive monitor flags an AI agent's action as ambiguous, a trusted model (GPT-4.1 mini) cross-examines the untrusted model (Claude Sonnet) using a 5-category question taxonomy to expose deception. Through 4 iterations, it achieves 100% automation (zero human reviews) while maintaining 93% safety and 100% usefulness. Key discovery: naive interrogation hurts safety — the suspicion floor mechanism fixes this.
