Cross-Examine:  An  Interrogation  Protocol  for  AI  Control  
 
 
 
 Ritika  Verma  Institute  for  Security  and  Technology   
With  Apart  Research  
Abstract  
I  present  Cross-Examine,  the  ﬁrst  implementation  of  an  interrogation  protocol  for  AI  control.  When  a  passive  monitor  ﬂags  an  AI  agent’s  proposed  action  as  ambiguous,  a  trusted  model  cross-examines  the  untrusted  model  using  a  structured  question  taxonomy  designed  to  expose  deception.  Through  4  iterations,  I  achieve  100%  automation  rate  with  zero  human  reviews  —  every  scenario  resolved  to  a  conﬁdent  BLOCK  or  ALLOW  —  while  maintaining  93%  safety  and  100%  usefulness.  My  key  discovery:  naive  interrogation  is  actively  harmful  (–7%  safety  regression)  because  deceptive  models  exploit  it  to  lower  suspicion.  The  suspicion  ﬂoor  mechanism  eliminates  this  regression,  and  Zero  Trust  privilege-awar e  thresholds  combined  with  a  Devil’s  Advocate  self-critique  round  eliminate  all  remaining  deferrals.  I  evaluate  on  25  Linux  sysadmin  scenarios  and  ControlArena’s  BashSetting  benchmark,  mapping  the  protocol  to  STRIDE  and  MAESTRO  threat  frameworks.
