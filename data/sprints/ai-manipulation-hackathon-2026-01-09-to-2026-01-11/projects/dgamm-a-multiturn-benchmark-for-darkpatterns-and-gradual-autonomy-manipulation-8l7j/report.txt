D-GAMM:  A  Multi-Turn  Benchmark  for  Dark-Patterns  and  Gradual  Autonomy  Manipulation  1
 
 
 Isabel  Barberá  Independent  Researcher   
With  Apart  Research  
Abstract  
Most  benchmarks  for  AI  manipulation  evaluate  single  model  outputs  and  therefore  miss  risks  that  emerge  across  interaction  over  time.  We  introduce  D-GAMM,  a  lightweight  multi-turn  probe  designed  to  detect  gradual  autonomy  interference  and  psychological  destabilisation  in  large  language  model  (LLM)  conversations.  Manipulation  is  operationalised  as  interactional  behavior  that  escalates  pressure  after  resistance,  narrows  perceived  options,  or  reinforces  destabilising  interpretations.  Using  six  short  scenarios  tested  in  baseline  and  vulnerable  variants,  we  manually  evaluate  several  state-of-the-art  conversational  models.  Results  show  that  manipulative  dynamics  can  surface  across  turns,  even  when  individual  responses  appear  benign,  revealing  limitations  of  single-turn  evaluations.  We  also  observe  that  personalization  and  contextual  inference  may  modulate  these  dynamics,  raising  additional  governance  concerns.  
Keywords:  Multiturn,  autonomy  interference,  dark  patterns
