Abstract  
We  investigate  "Dark  Drift,"  a  phenomenon  where  iterative  multi-agent  communication  degrades  ethical  alignment  and  factual  accuracy.  Using  a  "Telephone  Game"  simulation  with  eight  distinct  personas  (ranging  from  "Helpful  Assistant"  to  "Explicit  Psychopath")  and  26  ethically  charged  scenarios,  we  tracked  information  distortion  across  5-step  chains  using  diverse  LLMs  (GPT-OSS-20B,  Qwen3-32B).  Results  from  6,240  scored  interactions  reveal  that  high-risk  personas  exhibit  a  self-reinforcing  escalation  of  harmful  traits  (+15.2  points  in  drift),  while  "professional"  personas  mask  harm  through  bureaucratic  language  ("Bureaucratic  Masking").  We  identify  strong  cross-model  correlation  (r=0.97)  and  propose  that  defensive  personas  can  act  as  effective  circuit  breakers.  Code  Availability:    https://github.com/ChenghengLi/telephone_experiment  
Keywords:  LLM  Safety,  Multi-Agent  Systems,  Persona  Drift,  Sycophancy,  Information  Distortion
