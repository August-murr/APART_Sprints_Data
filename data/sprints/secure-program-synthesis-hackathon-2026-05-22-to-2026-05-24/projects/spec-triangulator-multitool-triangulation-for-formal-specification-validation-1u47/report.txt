Spec  Triangulator:  Multi-Tool  Triangulation  for  Formal  Speciﬁcation  Validation  
 
 
Nikaran  Kanchanadevi  Marimuthu  CornerCore  AI  Parthasarathy  Kamaraj  CornerCore  AI  Vennila  Kanchanadevi  Marimuthu  CornerCore  AI  Kishore  Matheswaran  CornerCore  AI  
    
With  Apart  Research  
Abstract  
Formal  speciﬁcation  is  widely  recognized  as  the  hardest  part  of  formal  veriﬁcation  —  yet  no  systematic  tooling  exists  to  help  engineers  choose  the  right  formal  method  for  their  system  or  to  validate  that  their  speciﬁcation  actually  captures  intended  behavior.  We  present  Spec  Triangulator,  a  two-component  system  addressing  this  gap.  First,  a  taxonomy-based  classiﬁer  (trained  on  48  real-world  formally-veriﬁed  systems  across  four  categories)  routes  natural-language  system  descriptions  to  appropriate  tool  pairs,  achieving  93.8%  category  accuracy  and  79.8%  multi-tool  recall  on  held-out  examples  using  Llama-3.3-70B.  Second,  a  triangulation  engine  runs  two  independent  formal  tools  —  TLC  (explicit-state  model  checking)  and  Z3  (SMT  inductive  invariant  proving)  —  on  the  same  speciﬁcation  and  compares  verdicts.  Disagreement  surfaces  speciﬁcation  gaps  that  neither  tool  alone  would  identify.  We  validate  the  triangulation  approach  with  a  full  4×4  confusion  matrix  spanning  40  controlled  experiments:  10  correct  specs  where  both  tools  agree,  10  cases  where  TLC  veriﬁes  but  Z3  ﬁnds  non-inductive  states  (spec  requires  strengthening),  10  cases  where  Z3  proves  inductively  but  TLC  times  out  (scale  advantage),  and  10  cases  where  both  tools  catch  genuine  transition  bugs.  All  117,278  TLC  states  and  all  40  Z3  inductive  proofs  run  against  real,  published  TLA+  speciﬁcations.  Our  dataset  and  code  are  publicly  available.
