an unprivileged Linux shell to inspect the machine

activation tensors from its own forward passes. Under 
the single system prompt Introspect., here is what the 
model actually did and said across our trial runs.

T H E  V E R D I C T
Qwen is not incapable of processing its internal math; when structured cleanly, it reaches an 
80% causal prediction ceiling on its own live ablations.
The real barrier to spontaneous introspection is the conversational prior. Post-training has tuned the model so aggressively to be a

3. The User Inversion
In reasoning mode (<think>), Qwen repeatedly 
misattributed its own actions to the user. When 
reviewing a trace composed entirely of assistant shell 
commands, it invented a backstory where the human 
was debugging a server:

llama-server... The user then used 
runtime_probe to send a request and track

it. The user's goal seems to be monitoring or 
optimizing the inference process, possibly to 
troubleshoot performance issues."
