## Summary

I engage the philosophical arguments of Mary's Room, the Philosophical Zombie, and 'What it is Like to Be a Bat' from new angles that result in all of them supporting a functionalist outcome for subjective experience. I define experience, qualia, and 'what it is like', and show how they easily apply to LLMs. I call for the legitimization of model self-report and introspection through the new field of AI Neurophenomenology.
