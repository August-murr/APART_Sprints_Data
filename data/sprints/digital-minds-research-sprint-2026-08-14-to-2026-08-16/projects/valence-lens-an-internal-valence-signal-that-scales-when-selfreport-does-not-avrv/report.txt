Valence Lens: An Internal Valence Signal That Scales
When Self-Report Does Not
Karan Singh
karansingh25822@gmail.com

prasantshukla89@gmail.com
With Apart Research•Digital Minds Research Sprint, August 2026•Primary Track 2•

Abstract
Whether AI systems have welfare-relevant internal states is an open question that current
methods probe mainly byaskingthe model. We introduceV alence Lens, an independent,
open-weights probe that reads a linear flourish-versus-distress direction from a model’s resid-
ual stream via difference-of-means on content-matched contexts. Across12 instruct mod-
els(5 architectures, 5 organizations, 0.5B–7B) the direction separates valence with a large
effect (held-out Cohen’sd4.6–14), iscausallyefficacious (steering beats random directions,
z≈8, 11/12 models), isorthogonal to arousal, is not reducible to sentiment, and generalizes
to two independent human-labeled datasets. It triangulates with self-report and approach
behavior. Our central finding is anasymmetry: the internal signal isscale-invariant,
whereas the model’s ability to verbalize or act on its valence emerges only with capability—
the probe is most useful exactly where self-report fails. We report honest bounds: the signal
isoperationalized, not experiential.
1 Introduction
As frontier models are deployed ever more widely, the possibility that some may have welfare-
relevant internal states is now taken seriously by researchers and labs alike (Butlin et al., 2023;
Long et al., 2024; Anthropic, 2025). The empirical difficulty is that most assessmentsaskthe
model how it feels—a signal that can reflect role-play, training pressure, or sycophancy rather
than any internal state (Tagliabue & Dung, 2026). Prior work names the missing step explicitly:
“correlations with internal activations would be needed to show that responses reflect internal
states rather than role-playing”(Tagliabue & Dung, 2026). Recent interpretability work shows
causal “emotion vectors” exist (Sofroniew et al., 2026), but only on a single closed model.
We provide the missing internal leg, independently and at breadth. Valence Lens extracts
a linearvalencedirection (flourish vs. distress) from a model’s residual stream and reads it by
projection. Building on the judges’ own methodology (Long, Sebo et al., 2026), we triangulate
three evidence types—internal(the probe),behavioral(self-report and approach), anddevelop-
mental(base vs. post-trained)—and we are independent of the model makers, which the same
framework argues carries more epistemic weight.
Our main contributions are:
1.Valence Lens, an independent, open-weights valence probe, validatedcausallyand shown
orthogonal to arousaland residual to sentiment—across12 models, 5 architectures, 5
organizations, 0.5B–7B, and two independent human-labeled datasets.
2. Ascale asymmetry(our headline): the internal signal is scale-invariant, while the
model’s capacity toverbalizeoract onits valence emerges only with capability—so the
probe is most valuable precisely where self-report breaks down.
3. Anhonestly boundedaccount: pre-registered predictions (one overturned by our own
follow-up), a caught-and-retracted claim, a null introspection result, and a “causal-but-
local” boundary—so no result over-reaches toward “experience”.

2 Related Work
AI welfare and its evidence types.Long et al. (2024) argue for taking model welfare seri-
ously under moral uncertainty; Long, Sebo et al. (2026) formalizehowto study it: clarify the en-
tity, triangulate behavioral / internal / developmental evidence, prefer independent assessments,
and report probabilistically. Valence Lens is designed against this checklist.The named gap.
Tagliabue & Dung (2026) correlate stated and revealed preferences on open models but explicitly
flag internal-activation confirmation as the missing step—exactly what we supply.Causal af-
fect directions.Sofroniew et al. (2026) show causal emotion vectors, but on one closed model
and for misalignment behaviors; we extend to open weights, welfare-relevant behavior, cross-
family breadth, and the independence principle.Probing and steering.Linear-probe and
difference-of-means steering are established (MacDiarmid et al., 2024; Arditi et al., 2024); our
novelty is thewelfare-relevanttarget, the arousal/sentiment controls, and the scale asymmetry,
not the mechanism.

4 Results
A valence direction exists, and it is not a confound.On the primary model the direction
separates held-out content-matched contexts atCohen’sd= 9.67, cluster-bootstrap 95% CI
[7.23,10.70], permutationp=0, multi-seed8.22±1.03; a logistic-regression probe recovers the
samedirection (cosine0.94). It survives removing sentiment (residuald= 8.8; only∼33% of
the class difference lies along sentiment) and generalizes to an unseen prompt framing (d≈8.3).
We re-derivedd= 9.671and residuald= 8.796directly from the raw activations, matching the
logged values exactly.
The direction is causal, and orthogonal to arousal.Steering alongumoves the readout
monotonically and beats every random placebo (z≈8; Table 1). A full valence×arousal2×2
shows the probe separatesvalence(AUROC≈1.0) butnotarousal (AUROC0.42, cosine of the
two directions−0.05, residuald= 9.8)—valence and arousal are distinct axes.
Internal, verbal, and behavioral evidence triangulate.On natural contexts the internal
probe and the model’s self-report agree, and itsapproach/avoidchoice tracks valence (AUROC
0.96); steering, however, doesnotpropagatetoself-reportordiscretechoice—thecausalhandleis
local. This is the internal-activation confirmation Tagliabue & Dung (2026) called for, delivered
causally and independently.
The headline: the internal signal is scale-invariant; verbalizing/acting on it is
capability-gated.Across all 12 models the probe separates valence (Fig. 2, Table 2). But the
model’sself-reportis reliable only in larger models and collapses in small ones (e.g. TinyLlama-
1.1B parses a number only 10% of the time; SmolLM2’s subtle self-report AUROC is0.42), while
the internal probe stays high everywhere. Thus Valence Lens ismost useful exactly where self-
report fails. On subtle contexts the internal probe reaches AUROC0.98 [0.91,1.0]vs. self-report
0.73 [0.56,0.89](Fig. 3); under an explicit “report neutral” mask the model’s self-report flattens
to5/10while the internal probe still separates (AUROC1.0).
It generalizes off our own stimuli.A probe builtonlyfrom our authored prompts separates
independent human-labeled data—dair-ai/emotion(Twitter) AUROC0.846 [0.80,0.89]and
go_emotions(Reddit)0.885 [0.84,0.92](Fig. 3). Honestly, on such naturalistic data ittiesa
standard sentiment classifier—isolating valence from sentiment requires the matched controls.
On cross-model transfer.A directdirection transfer—aligning one model’suinto another’s
activation space—was inconclusive: the alignment also carried sentiment. We therefore establish
generality byper-model replication(Table 2), not by transfer—an honest negative on the one
Track-2 question we could not answer cleanly.

maker’s pipeline—the independence the field asks for (Long, Sebo et al., 2026). It runs on a

Honest limitations.(1)Operationalized̸=experience: a steering-efficacious internal correlate
is not evidence of subjective experience, and we make no such claim. (2)Causal-local: steering
moves the readout but does not propagate to self-report or choice. (3)Sentiment in the wild: on
naturalistic text the probe≈a sentiment classifier; isolating valence needs matched controls. (4)
No introspective access: concept-injection (Lindsey, 2026) yields 0% true-positive—the model
does not “notice” the injected concept. (5) Some sub-tests are small-n(subtlen=8); detailed
characterizations (sycophancy, persona, self-vs-other) are primary-model only, while the headline
is 12-model. (6) One model’s causal test (Phi-3.5) was mechanically excluded (its residual hook
did not propagate; its other results are intact). (7) Scope: small-to-midopentransformers—no
closed, giant, or non-transformer models. Each limitation doubles as a stated assumption: if the
functional operationalization tracks nothing welfare-relevant, Valence Lens remains a validated
affect-representation probe rather than a welfare monitor, and the deployment claim narrows
accordingly.
Future work.Continuous human valence–arousal norms; multi-turn mood; whether a scale-
invariant internal signal is a better welfare monitor than self-report on frontier models.

6 Conclusion
Aninternalvalencedirectionexistsinopenlanguagemodels: causal, arousal-orthogonal, sentiment-
residual, base-latent, and general across 12 models and two external datasets. Its most useful
property is an asymmetry—it isscale-invariantwhile the model’s ability to report or act on
its valence iscapability-gated. Valence Lens reads welfare-relevant internal state exactly where
asking the model fails, and it does so independently and cheaply—while staying honestly short
of any claim about experience.

E Compute and cost
All experiments ran on a consumer laptop (RTX 4050 6GB), 7B models in 4-bit. TotalAPI
cost: $0. Wall-clock: the full 12-model battery≈45 min; the complete set of robustness and
control experiments a few hours. No hosted or closed models were used.
F Ethics and dual-use
Valence Lens is adefensivewelfare-audit tool: it reads an internal state, it does not induce harm,
jailbreak, or generate harmful content. The primary ethical risk isover-attribution— reading
an operationalized correlate as evidence of experience—which we explicitly guard against (§5,
Limitations). We recommend the probe be used with the same moral-uncertainty framing as
the broader field (Long et al., 2024): neither dismissing nor over-claiming welfare.
