Abstract  
The  EU  AI  Act  requires  providers  of  General  Purpose  AI  (GPAI)  models  to  submit  technical  documentation  under  Article  53,  following  the  GPAI  Code  of  Practice.  This  process  is  traditionally  manual:  model  providers  must  read  through  their  model  cards,  cross-reference  compliance  requirements,  and  populate  a  Word  document  template  ﬁeld  by  ﬁeld.  This  project  presents  a  Model  Context  Protocol  (MCP)  server  that  automates  this  workﬂow.  Given  a  Hugging  Face  model  ID,  the  tool  fetches  the  model  card,  follows  linked  resources  (e.g.,  arXiv  papers  or  PDFs)  to  gather  additional  context,  retrieves  the  ofﬁcial  compliance  questions,  uses  Claude  to  extract  and  map  relevant  information,  and  generates  a  completed  .docx  compliance  form  for  submission  to  the  EU  AI  Ofﬁce.  The  tool  is  deployable  locally  or  via  cloud  platforms  (e.g.,  Railway)  and  integrates  with  Claude  as  a  custom  connector.  This  work  directly  addresses  Track  2  (Compliance  Infrastructure  &  Privacy-Preserving  Proofs)  by  providing  compliance  automation  tooling  for  EU  AI  Act  reporting  requirements.  
Keywords:  EU  AI  Act,  GPAI,  compliance  automation,  Article  53,  MCP  server,  regulatory  infrastructure,  HuggingFace
