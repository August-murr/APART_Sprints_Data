Abstract  
As  frontier  AI  models  accelerate  toward  critical  capability  thresholds,  the  landscape  of  safety  evaluation  remains  fragmented  and  opaque.  While  major  labs  like  OpenAI,  Anthropic,  and  Google  DeepMind  have  published  safety  frameworks  (e.g.,  Preparedness  Framework,  RSP,  FSF),  they  utilize  disparate  terminologies  and  metrics,  creating  a  "Tower  of  Babel"  that  obscures  the  aggregate  risk  level  of  the  industry.  
Line4 addresses  this  visibility  gap  by  functioning  as  an  operational  "watchtower"  for  AI  risk.  Inspired  by  the  call  for  veriﬁable  red  lines  at  the  CSEAI  Hackathon,  we  developed  a  uniﬁed  dashboard  that  ingests,  normalizes,  and  visualizes  safety  data  across  four  critical  domains:  CBRN  (Chemical,  Biological,  Radiological,  Nuclear),  Cyber  Offense,  Autonomous  Replication,  and  Deceptive  Alignment.  Our  approach  utilizes  an  automated  intelligence  pipeline  to  scrape  System  Cards  and  map  qualitative  risk  levels  (e.g.,  "ASL-2"  or  "Medium")  onto  a  standardized  0–100  risk  scale.  The  result  is  a  real-time  visualization  of  the  "distance  to  danger,"  transforming  abstract  safety  commitments  into  hard,  trackable  metrics  for  policymakers  and  independent  auditors.

to

passive

 Hackathon  Scope:  For  this  demonstration,  we  utilized  a  mix  of  real  extracted  data  and  projected  
"future
