 1  Creating  standardized  AI  safety  evaluations   that  run  in  a  hardened  &  private  way

Abstract  
Post-compute,  non-inference  time  AI  safety  (e.g.  evals,  benchmarks,  automated  red-teaming)  lacks  a  uniﬁed  standardized  infrastructure  suitable  for  international  collaboration.  Results  aren't  comparable  across  frameworks.  Reproducibility  depends  on  undocumented  assumptions.  Trust  requires  transparency  that  current  tooling  doesn't  provide.  
We  built  a  working  prototype  for  creating  streamlined,  standardized  AI  safety  evaluations,  benchmarks  &  automated  red-teaming  with  a  private,  secure  &  isolated  environment  to  run  them.  The  architecture  consists  of  a  sealed  controller  that  serves  as  the  only  external  dependency  for  evaluation  implementation,  enabling  streamlined  development.  This  provides  separation  of  concern  by  handling  environment  orchestration,  air-gapped  execution  and  isolated,  private  model  interfacing  &  containerization  separately  from  the  evaluation  code.  
International  AI  safety  collaboration  needs  common  infrastructure  that  is  collaborative,  transparent,  neutral,  veriﬁable  &  privacy-preserving  for  sensitive  IP.  Not  to  replace  existing  initiatives,  but  to  unite  them.  This  template  is  a  working  step  toward  that  mission.
