Executive Summary
AEGIS (Automated Enforcement & Global Inspection System) resolves the ”Trust
Deadlock” in international AI governance. By utilizing Simulated Trusted Execution
Environments (TEEs), the protocol enables regulatory bodies to perform ”blind” in-
spections of frontier AI models. This allows for the verification of compliance with the
1025 FLOP compute threshold and CBRN safety guardrails without the need for labs
to expose proprietary intellectual property or national security assets.
1. The Problem: The Verification Gap
The global community faces a critical implementation hurdle in the enforcement of frame-
works like the EU AI Act:Excludability vs. Verification.
•Intellectual Property Risks:Labs cannot grant regulators direct access to clusters
without risking massive IP exfiltration.
•National Security:Sovereignty concerns prevent cross-border physical inspections
of high-performance compute clusters.
•Trust Deadlock:Governance currently relies on ”self-reporting,” which lacks credibil-
ity in an adversarial international climate.
2. The Solution: AEGIS Protocol
AEGIS shifts the verification paradigm from ”High Trust / Low Verification” to”Zero Trust /
High Verification.”Instead of the regulator taking the data, the regulator sends theInspec-
tion Codeto the data.
2.1 Technical Pillars
1.The Blind Inspector:A containerized safety evaluation that executes inside the Lab’s
secure hardware perimeter.
2.Cryptographic Attestation:Generates a SHA-256 hash of the execution log, provid-
ing proof that the audit occurred on attested hardware without revealing the model’s
internal weights.

4. Methodology & Execution
The AEGIS prototype is built using a React/Node.js stack (or Python/Streamlit) to simulate
the three-party interaction between the Lab, the Regulator, and the Auditor.
•Simulation:We mocked a TEE where model metadata is stored as encrypted objects.
•Verification Engine:Performs a ”Blind Check” against the EU threshold.
•Output:Generates a cryptographically signed ”Compliance Certificate” (PDF) con-
taining the proof hash.
5. Impact and Theory of Change
AEGIS turns AI governance from a diplomatic negotiation into a mathematical certainty. It
allows nations to sign binding treaties knowing that verification is automated and privacy-
preserving. This is the technical foundation required for the next generation of international
AI safety agreements.
