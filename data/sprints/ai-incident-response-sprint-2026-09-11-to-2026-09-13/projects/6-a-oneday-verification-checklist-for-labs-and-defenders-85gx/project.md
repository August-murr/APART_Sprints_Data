## Summary

This project investigates the July 2026 event.?... The attack on OpenAI–Hugging Face highlights the significant security loopholes that enabled it to propagate across diverse environments. Moreover, We created a one-day defensive verification checklist that covered network egress, credential isolation, least privilege, trust boundaries, detection-to-response, and other critical areas based on the documented attack chain. The inclusion of a distinct verification question and pass condition in each check allows labs and defenders to independently evaluate the security of their AI-agent environments.
