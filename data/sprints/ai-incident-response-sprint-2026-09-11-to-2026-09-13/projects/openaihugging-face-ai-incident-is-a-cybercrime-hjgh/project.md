### Review 1

This research eloquantly points out a common false belief that current laws do not apply to AI systems. It evaluates what happened during the OpenAI/Huggingface incident under South African law and outlines precedent cases outside of cyber as well that these events are crimes under these laws. It fails to address that the US has similar laws like the CFAA and precedent cases in the realm of cyber.
