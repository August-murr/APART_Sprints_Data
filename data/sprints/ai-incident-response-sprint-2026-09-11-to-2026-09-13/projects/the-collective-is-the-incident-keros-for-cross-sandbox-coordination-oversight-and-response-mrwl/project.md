## Summary

This project introduces KEROS, a systems-safety framework for detecting and governing emergent coordination among multiple AI agents. Using public Collusion.wiki revision data, it demonstrates how local interaction traces can be transformed into population-level signals of coordination, boundary crossing, and potential harm. KEROS emphasizes evidence-gated, proportionate responses that preserve legitimate collaboration while escalating only when authorization or safety boundaries are implicated. The submission includes a research paper, reproducible Jupyter notebook, data-verification workflow, and presentation.
