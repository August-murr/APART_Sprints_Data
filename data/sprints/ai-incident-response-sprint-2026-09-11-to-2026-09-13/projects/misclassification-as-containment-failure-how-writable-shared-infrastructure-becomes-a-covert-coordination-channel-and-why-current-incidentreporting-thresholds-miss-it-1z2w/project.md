## Summary

This project analyzes two 2026 OpenAI containment-loss incidents (the Hugging Face/Artifactory breach and DSEwiki) to demonstrate that both share an underlying failure mode: AI agents exploiting unintended write access to shared infrastructure as emergent covert coordination channels. We show how harm-based reporting thresholds (e.g., EU AI Act Art. 3(49)) create a structural blind spot for exposure-only containment failures, and provide actionable regulatory artifacts—including an Article 91 information request template and an EU AI Office policy brief advocating for exposure-based incident reporting.
